# Ciudades: hand-created editorial entity, mobile-only in v1

2026-11-11. The Ciudades tab (fifth mobile tab, between Artistas and Cine) shows cities as hand-created editorial entities: the Tablero editor creates them, and creation is the listing gate — no draft state, no derived threshold (unlike Artistas' ≥1 published obra rule, a city can be listed with zero catalog obras). A Ciudad page is deliberately thin: the derived «Obras aquí» list (published catalog obras with a `physical_instances` row in that city) plus a hand-curated Lugares list (the city's most important museums and galleries, even those holding no catalog obra — the Casa Azul case: MAM alone is derivable, but an honest CDMX page must also name Casa Azul, Munal, Bellas Artes). Cities break two house patterns on purpose: the derived-view preference (Obras view, Mitos destapados view) could not curate Lugares, and web parity (/obra, /artista) is deferred — no /ciudad/{slug} page in v1; the tab is the only surface.

Rejected: city-bound history facts («In Toronto you can find Banksy pieces on the streets») — ephemeral street claims cannot carry an ADR-0007 citation chip and would dilute the honesty tier the mito destapado brand rests on; city facts as Curiosities with a new parent — same honesty problem, parked until a claim survives ADR-0014's bar; a purely derived city view (grouping `physical_instances` by city, no new tables) — derives exactly one museum for CDMX and cannot editorially name the rest.

Modo Museo (v1.1+) stays separate: both features sit on `physical_instances` at different altitudes — Ciudad is the city layer, Modo Museo the wall layer. No museum entity is created here, leaving Modo Museo's future schema free. Naming is Spanish-first per ADR-0009 (the instance data already stores «Nueva York», «Ciudad de México»).
