# Curiosities are typed and sourced

2026-09-29. Every curiosity carries a type (fact | mito destapado | leyenda) and a source (URL + note, dashboard-visible; one clean chip in-app, not a forty-footnote wall). Mito destapado — the debunk — is the brand verb as a premium, shareable tier; leyenda renders honestly as "Se cuenta que…". The 20–30 min verification budget per Story is non-negotiable: sourced + typed + curated + Spanish is the trust combination Wikipedia doesn't offer.
