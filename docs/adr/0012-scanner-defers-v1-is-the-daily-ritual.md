# The scanner defers; v1 is the daily ritual (phase plan)

2026-09-29. Defended and accepted in the round-4 hearing. The scanner is an acquisition/demo feature, not retention — nobody walks past paintings daily, so scan-frequency can't be a habit loop. Retention is obra del día + weekly episode + push: the Daily Art mechanic with better content, on infra (push) already built for Avísame. If 30 great Stories don't retain, the scanner wouldn't have saved it.

**Phase 1 — La Fundación** (weeks 1–3): dashboard, schema, import, 10 Stories drafted. Exit: end-to-end publish works.

**Phase 2 — La Biblioteca** (weeks 4–12): app + public pages + launch. Exit: 30 published + 20 in pipeline, 10 episodes in the can, Play closed beta passed (incl. privacy, ADR-0017), attribution live.

**Phase 3 — El Escáner**, gated on ALL four (dashboard readiness widget tracks live): catalog ≥ 50 published; reference vectors on 100% of the scannable corpus; installs ≥ 500; D7 retention ≥ 15% — the honest gate that refuses the fun feature while the core is sick.

Rider: human reference-gathering (5–7 diverse Commons files) stays in enriched — already budgeted in the 75 h; vector embedding is a deferred Phase-3 batch script over image_assets (minutes of compute, zero editorial hours); story-only works skip gathering entirely. Two launch moments, one catalog: launch end-cards say "la historia completa está en la app"; "escanéalo" arrives with Phase 3, and high-performing old episodes get re-issued with the new card.
